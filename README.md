## Apps & Online Platforms
- [HelloChinese](https://www.hellochinese.cc/) [B–I] [F] — Comprehensive app for speaking, listening, reading, and writing.
- [Duolingo Chinese](https://www.duolingo.com/course/zh/en/Learn-Chinese) [B] [F] — Gamified basics and daily practice.
- [LingoDeer](https://www.lingodeer.com/) [B–I] [$] — Structured lessons with grammar focus.
- [ChineseSkill](https://www.chineseskill.com/) [B] [F] — Beginner-friendly courses and practice.
- [TypingMandarin](https://www.typingmandarin.com/) [B–A] [F] — Dictation, typing challenges, and atomic writing exercises.
- [Skritter](https://skritter.com/) [B–A] [$] — Character writing and spaced repetition for Hanzi.
- [Yoyo Chinese](https://yoyochinese.com/) [B–A] [$] — Video-based curriculum emphasizing real-life usage.
- [ChinesePod](https://chinesepod.com/) [B–A] [$] — Podcast lessons with transcripts and exercises.
- [SubChinese](https://subchinese.com/) [I–A] [F] — Turns a Chinese video into pinyin subtitles with an English line and a clickable dictionary.
## Textbooks & Graded Readers
- [Integrated Chinese](https://www.cheng-tsui.com/browse/integrated-chinese) (Cheng & Tsui) [B–I] [$] — Widely used course with workbook and audio.
- [New Practical Chinese Reader](https://www.blcup.com/) [B–I] [$] — Classic series with dialogues and exercises.
- [Mandarin Companion Graded Readers](https://mandarincompanion.com/) [B–I] [$] — Learner-friendly graded stories.
- [Chengyu/Idiom Readers](https://www.hackingchinese.com/a-guide-to-chinese-idioms-chengyu/) [I–A] [$] — Explore fixed expressions and cultural context.
- [Children's and Bilingual Books](https://www.betterchineselearning.com/) [B] [$] — Early reading with pictures and parallel text.
## Dictionaries & Reference
- [Pleco](https://www.pleco.com/) [B–A] [F] — Mobile dictionary with OCR, handwriting, example sentences.
- [Hanping Dictionary](https://www.hanpingchinese.com/) [B–A] [F] — Android dictionary with tone colors and audio.
- [MDBG](https://www.mdbg.net/) / [LINE Dict](https://dict.naver.com/linedict/zhendict/dict.html) / [YellowBridge](https://www.yellowbridge.com/chinese/dictionary.php) [B–A] [F] — Web dictionaries and character etymology.
## Audio & Video
- [Yoyo Chinese YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@YoyoChinese) [B–A] [F] — Free videos complementing courses.
- [Slow Chinese](https://www.slowchinese.net/) / [ChinesePod Podcasts](https://chinesepod.com/podcasts) [B–A] [F] — Levelled listening practice.
- [Movies, TV dramas, C-dramas, and music](https://www.viki.com/tv/chinese) [I–A] [F] — Use with subtitles for immersion.
## Courses & Tutoring
- [University Classes](https://online-learning.harvard.edu/course/introduction-chinese-language-and-culture) [B–A] [$] — Structured curriculum and assessment.
- [Confucius Institutes & Community Centers](https://www.chinese.cn/) [B–I] [$] — Local/remote classes.
- [iTalki](https://www.italki.com/) / [Preply](https://preply.com/) [B–A] [$] — 1:1 lessons with native tutors.
## Typing, Writing & Calligraphy
- [Skritter](https://skritter.com/) [B–A] [$] — Stroke order, handwriting practice, retention.
- [TypingMandarin](https://www.typingmandarin.com/) [B–A] [F] — Pinyin/zhuyin typing drills and dictation.
- [Make Me a Hanzi](https://www.skishore.me/makemeahanzi/) / [AnimCJK](https://github.com/parsimonhi/animCJK) [B–A] [F] — Stroke animations and decomposition.
## SRS & Flashcards
