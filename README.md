## Apps & Online Platforms
- HelloChinese [B–I] [F] — Comprehensive app for speaking, listening, reading, and writing.
- Duolingo Chinese [B] [F] — Gamified basics and daily practice.
- LingoDeer [B–I] [$] — Structured lessons with grammar focus.
- ChineseSkill [B] [F] — Beginner-friendly courses and practice.
- TypingMandarin [B–A] [F] — Dictation, typing challenges, and atomic writing exercises.
- Skritter [B–A] [$] — Character writing and spaced repetition for Hanzi.
- Yoyo Chinese [B–A] [$] — Video-based curriculum emphasizing real-life usage.
- ChinesePod [B–A] [$] — Podcast lessons with transcripts and exercises.

## Textbooks & Graded Readers
- Integrated Chinese (Cheng & Tsui) [B–I] [$] — Widely used course with workbook and audio.
- New Practical Chinese Reader [B–I] [$] — Classic series with dialogues and exercises.
- Mandarin Companion Graded Readers [B–I] [$] — Learner-friendly graded stories.
- Chengyu/Idiom Readers [I–A] [$] — Explore fixed expressions and cultural context.
- Children’s and Bilingual Books [B] [$] — Early reading with pictures and parallel text.

## Tips
- Mix resources: combine input (listening/reading) with output (speaking/writing).
- Track consistency with SRS and short daily goals; prioritize tone accuracy.
- Prefer level-appropriate content; increase difficulty gradually.
